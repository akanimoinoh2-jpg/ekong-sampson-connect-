Theme 01

Education and Skills

Problem

Low quality access, uneven school support, weak youth skills pipelines.

Proposal

Improve school support, teacher capacity, digital learning and technical skills pathways.

Impact

Better learning outcomes and stronger local human capital.

Theme 02

Infrastructure and Utilities

Problem

Uneven road access, weak power reliability and poor public amenities in some communities.

Proposal

Prioritize road upgrades, utilities, drainage and basic public works in underserved areas.

Impact

Improved connectivity, productivity and service delivery.

Theme 03

Healthcare Access

Problem

Weak community health coverage, limited facilities and gaps in preventive care access.

Proposal

Strengthen primary care, outreach and local health infrastructure across the district.

Impact

Healthier families and reduced treatment burdens.

Theme 04

Youth, Jobs and Civic Participation

Problem

Young people face limited opportunities and weak pathways into productive engagement.

Proposal

Expand apprenticeships, community enterprise support and youth leadership platforms.

Impact

Greater economic participation and stronger civic energy.

Theme 05

Agriculture and Food Security

Problem

Food systems remain fragile in parts of the district, with uneven farmer support and value-chain gaps.

Proposal

Support local producers, storage, market access and food safety practices.

Impact

Stronger rural livelihoods and more resilient communities.

Theme 06

Enterprise and MSMEs

Problem

Local small businesses need better access to capital, branding and market visibility.

Proposal

Build support around SME financing, business development and local value creation.

Impact

More jobs, stronger local commerce and inclusive growth.

Theme 07

Security and Civic Trust

Problem

Communities need safety, stronger policing coordination and confidence in public institutions.

Proposal

Improve local cooperation, secure public spaces and strengthen accountability mechanisms.

Impact

Safer communities and more trust in public systems.

Theme 08

Social Development

Problem

Some communities feel disconnected from public institutions and service delivery.

Proposal

Increase outreach, transparent engagement and community-focused development planning.

Impact

More responsive governance and better public trust.
