01 / About Ekong Sampson

A career in public service.

His career spans local government, state legislative and executive service, and representation in the National Assembly.

Profile at a glance

Senator Ekong Sampson, Ph.D, is an accomplished journalist, author, poet, publisher, legal practitioner and public servant whose career has been defined by intellectual depth, impactful leadership and an enduring commitment to public service and human capital development.

Early life and education

Born on September 21, 1966, Senator Sampson hails from Mkpat Enin Local Government Area of Akwa Ibom State. His academic journey reflects a relentless pursuit of excellence.

He obtained an Ordinary National Diploma (OND) in Mass Communication with Distinction from The Polytechnic, Calabar, in 1986, before proceeding to study law. He earned his LL.B degree from the University of Cross River State in 1990, attended the Nigerian Law School, Lagos, where he obtained the Barrister-at-Law (BL) qualification in 1991, and later secured a Master of Laws (LL.M) degree from the University of Lagos in 1999. In 2020, he crowned his academic accomplishments with a Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D) in Law from the University of Calabar.

Legal practice and legislative apprenticeship

Senator Sampson's professional career commenced in legal practice. Between 1992 and 1996, he worked with Pat Okupa & Co. in Benin City and subsequently became the Managing Partner of Abalu, Bobga, Sampson & Co., Legal Practitioners, Ikeja, Lagos, where he practised from 1998 to 2000. His expertise and legislative acumen led to his appointment as Chief Legislative/Special Assistant to Senator Udoma Udo Udoma, then Chairman of the Senate Committee on Appropriations, a position he held from 2000 to 2003.

Local government and state legislature

His foray into elective politics began in 2003 when he was elected Councilor and Chairman of Mkpat Enin Local Government Area, serving two successful terms until 2007. He subsequently represented Mkpat Enin State Constituency in the Akwa Ibom State House of Assembly from 2007 to 2015, where he distinguished himself as one of the Assembly's most visible, active and respected lawmakers.

Service in the Akwa Ibom State Executive Council

From 2015 to 2020, Senator Sampson served in the Akwa Ibom State Executive Council, holding several strategic positions, including Commissioner for Rural Development; Economic Development, Labour and Manpower Planning; Environment and Petroleum Resources; and Special Adviser in the Bureau of Political and Legislative Affairs and Water Resources. Across these portfolios, he demonstrated exceptional administrative competence and a people-centred approach to governance.

Education and human capital development

A passionate advocate of education and intellectual development, Senator Sampson sponsors the monthly meeting of the Uyo Book Club, a vibrant intellectual community that promotes reading and scholarly engagement. He also established Excellence College, Mkpat Enin, in 2014, a notable educational institution that provided tuition-free education to many students for several years before its eventual takeover by the Akwa Ibom State Government.

Empowerment and constituency impact

Throughout his public life, Senator Sampson has earned a reputation as a quiet champion of empowerment and human capital development. He has facilitated employment opportunities for over 200 Akwa Ibom youths and, since assuming office as Senator in June 2023, has facilitated federal government employment for more than 50 constituents.

He has also supported numerous individuals and communities through the donation of vehicles, scholarships, grants, parcels of land, medical assistance and welfare packages, particularly to party faithfuls and the less privileged. He is also credited with initiating and championing the construction of the People's Democratic Party Secretariat in Mkpat Enin Local Government Area, regarded as one of the finest local government party secretariats in Nigeria.

Election to the Nigerian Senate

In February 2023, Senator Ekong Sampson was elected to represent Akwa Ibom South Senatorial District in the 10th Senate of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. He currently serves as Chairman of the Senate Committee on Solid Minerals Development, Vice Chairman of the Senate Committee on Basic and Secondary Education, and is a member of several other strategic committees. He is also the Chairman of the Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus.

Legislative record

As a legislator, Senator Sampson has distinguished himself as a widely respected voice in the Senate, noted for his legislative depth, incisive contributions and forward-looking perspectives. He has sponsored several significant bills and motions, including the establishment of the Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi, the Federal Medical Centre Act (Amendment), the Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket, the Solid Minerals Development Corporation, and the South South Development Commission, among others.

Personal life

Known for his simplicity, humility, accessibility and vast knowledge base, Senator Ekong Sampson remains a respected statesman and an influential figure in Nigeria's legislative and political landscape.

He is happily married to Mrs. Matina Sampson, a senior staff member of the National Assembly, and their union is blessed with children.
