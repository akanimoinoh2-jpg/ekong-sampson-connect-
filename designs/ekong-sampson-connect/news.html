Ikot Essen Akpan Ntuen Commends Senator Ekong Sampson over Ibekwe–Ikot Unya–Ekpuk Road, Solar-Powered Water Project

Residents of Ikot Essen Akpan Ntuen community in Ikpa Ikono Clan, Mkpat Enin Local Government Area of Akwa Ibom State have lauded the Senator representing Akwa Ibom South, Ekong Sampson, for his impactful interventions in the area, particularly the ongoing Ibekwe–Ikot Unya–Ekpuk Road project and a newly commissioned solar-powered water scheme.
