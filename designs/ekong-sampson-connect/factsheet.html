Senate Fact Sheet

Accomplishments and interventions in the Senate.

A concise fact sheet of Senator Ekong Sampson's accomplishments and interventions in the Senate.

Appointments

  • Chairman, Senate Committee on Solid Minerals
  • Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on Education (Basic & Secondary)
  • Formerly, Vice Chairman, Senate Committee on FCT
  • Chairman, Akwa Ibom National Assembly Caucus
  • Member of a number of other Senate Committees
  • Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to Investigate Abuse of Fire Arms by Officers of Nigeria Customs
  • Member, Senate Ad Hoc Committee to investigate Alleged illegal annexure of Nigerian mangroves communities by the Republic of Cameroun
  • Senate Ad-hoc Committee to investigate Illegal Oil Theft in Nigeria

Bills

  • Bill for the establishment of a Federal Medical Centre in Akwa Ibom South. (Bill as part of the FMC Act Amendment, set for implementation)
  • Bill for Establishment of Federal University of Medical Sciences, Eket (Set for Second Reading)
  • Bill for establishment of Federal University of Technology, Ikot Abasi (set for Third Reading)
  • Bills for the establishment of the Nigerian Solid Minerals Development Corporation (First Reading)
  • Child Rights Act Amendment Bill (First Reading)
  • Bill for the establishment of the South South Development Commission (co-sponsor) — Passed and Assented
  • Mining Sites remediation and reclamation Bill (First Reading), among others.

Motions (Sponsored & co-sponsored)

  • Urgent need for the reconstruction of Calabar – Itu Highway
  • Need for Increased Security Surveillance along Akwa Ibom Coastal Highway
  • Urgent Need to investigate Alleged annexation of Nigerian mangroves communities by the Republic of Cameroun
  • Notification on the Transition of Senator Joseph Wayas, former President of the Senate
  • Call for Urgent Rehabilitation of Federal Government Roads Across the country, among many others.
  • Matter of Urgent National Importance: The heart-rending hijack of a boat and abduction of passengers, including prospective JAMB candidates, by sea pirates along the Calabar–Oron waterways
  • Many Bills and Motions are also filed to be taken.

Petitions

  • Over 9 petitions presented for constituents by Senator Ekong Sampson. Two of the petitions have led to reinstatement into Federal Government employment for the concerned constituents. Senator Sampson has equally used his good office to mediate alternative settlements for many constituents with their employers.

Facilitation of Federal Government Employment

True to his well-known love for sustainable human capital development, Senator Ekong Sampson has facilitated employment and reinstatement for over 60 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South and Akwa Ibom State into top agencies, parastatals and in the uniform organisations.

  • He has also facilitated human capacity development training for over 1000 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South, in areas including mining, media, ICT, among others.

Empowerment

  • A Mega empowerment programme held in his first six months in office had over 1000 indigenes of Akwa Ibom South as beneficiaries, mostly youth and women. While many received education and business support grants, some constituents received cars.
  • A second tranche of the empowerment is billed to hold later this year, with improved packages and increased beneficiaries.
  • Provision of 100 million naira grant to students in Akwa Ibom South
  • Announcement of an additional 10 million naira for Law Students from Akwa Ibom South in Nigeria Law Schools
  • Senator Sampson's massive free medical outreach is equally underway, this year.
  • Periodic donation of quality car gifts, Tricycles and motorcycles to supporters and constituents
  • Over 50 physical projects facilitated to Akwa Ibom South

Other Advocacies

Senator Ekong Sampson has been a key advocate for the:

  • Establishment of the South South Development Commission
  • Revitalization of ALSCON
  • Construction of Roads in Akwa Ibom South
  • Establishment of the Federal University of Agriculture, in Abak, Akwa Ibom State
  • Ibom Deep Seaport
  • Completion of Nkari Dam

Working Collaborations

  • The Onomkpoinam Mkpat Enin enjoys active collaboration with the Senate President, Senator Godswill Akpabio, Governor Umo Eno, fellow National Assembly members, Ministers, relevant MDAs and a wide strata of stakeholders in Akwa Ibom South and beyond.
  • Widely respected in the National Assembly as a bridge builder, deep intellect, range and maturity.
